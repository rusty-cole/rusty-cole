## Customer Success Retriever

Every CSM I know has dug through old files looking for one template, rebuilt a success plan from scratch, or reused the last one with new names. I built Customer Success Retriever to make that work faster.

It helps create:

- Lifecycle emails tailored to the account stage
- Renewal risk briefs with cited evidence
- Pre-call account research
- Executive summaries
- QBR outlines and success plans
- Custom playbooks and account strategies

I have revised it repeatedly until it produces work I would send to a real customer.
